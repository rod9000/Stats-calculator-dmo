Sakuyamon: Miko Mode stands as a symbol of grace, wisdom, and spiritual enlightenment within the Digital World. Adorned in flowing robes of pure white, embellished with intricate patterns reminiscent of ancient Japanese traditions, Sakuyamon exudes an aura of serenity and inner peace. The gentle rustle of her garments echoes the tranquility of a sacred temple, as she moves with elegant poise and purpose.

Her celestial staff, the "Divine Rod," is crafted from the finest cherry wood and adorned with delicate blossoms that bloom eternally. With it, she channels the ancient powers of nature and the divine, wielding them with grace and precision to protect the innocent and uphold the balance of the Digital World.

Kuzuhamon - Miko Mode was added to Korean Digimon Masters on May 14, 2024.

Overview

Pros:

  • Removing Debuff Skill (can be even used during silence debuff)
  • Gives your allies exslusive effects that increase Critical Damage, Skill Damage and decrease Receved Damage.
  • Can be applied Extreme Evolution buff 25%.
  • Very High Critical chance.
  • Has a exclusive title +500 Hit Rate, +2000 HP, +15% Darkness Attribute Skill Damage.
  • Unlocks a Deck Buff applying 17% AS, 80% Critical Damage for 10 seconds (10% chance), 80% Skill Damage for 10 seconds (10% chance).
  • Being Burst Mode means she can use the stronger memory skills.

Cons:

  • Locked behind a ~$3,600 data summon pity with a 0.05% drop rate on pulls.
  • Stats below other Digimon in its category.
  • Slow Skill Animations
  • Now you need two Mikos to run endgame content to get the same F4 effect duration as with one Miko before this change
  • F2 is now an AOE which means it is an issue inside Chimeramon Dungeon. On top of losing a lot of base damage.
