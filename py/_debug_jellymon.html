Jellymon is a Mollusk Digimon. It is a Digimon with a squishy head like a jellyfish. Its whole body is flexible, which allows it to retract its body into its head, and it usually lives with just its head showing. When it is just its head, it is light enough to fly just by being blown by the wind. It prefers beautiful environments and actions, and once it sees foul behavior, it frees its body in anger and turns into its Battle Mode. It has countless tentacles.
